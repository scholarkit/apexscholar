## Features

3. Interactive Visualizations
4. Backup & Restore
5. Citation Engine
6. Unified Papers Explorer (ArXiv, Semantic Scholar, openAlex, Google Scholar)

## Roadmap

We are constantly working to bridge the gap between general data management and the specific workflows of academic researchers. Here is what is on the horizon:

### Literature & Citation Management
- **Reference Syncing:** Native integrations with Zotero, Mendeley, and EndNote APIs.

- **PDF Workspace:** Upload papers, highlight text, and link annotations directly to your structured database.

### Deep AI Integrations
- **Document Q&A (RAG):** "Chat with your PDFs" to instantly summarize methodologies or findings across multiple uploaded papers.
- **Auto-Extraction:** AI-assisted extraction of tables, $p$-values, and sample sizes from literature into your data entry fields.
- **Smart Recommendations:** Automated literature discovery powered by OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar.

### Grant Management
- **Grant & Budget Tracker:** Dedicated modules for tracking funding spend and grant deliverables.

### Collaboration & Open Science
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Distinct permissions for Principal Investigators (PIs), Post-Docs, and Research Assistants.
- **Data Publishing:** A 1-click toggle to make specific datasets or visualizations "Public" for easy sharing as supplementary materials.
